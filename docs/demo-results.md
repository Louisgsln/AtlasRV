> The table and images below were generated by the original v0.1 allocation.
> AtlasRV v0.2 adds false-discovery control, correlation-aware allocation, explicit
> holding costs, and regime attribution. Re-run the command at the bottom to create
> current results for the checked-out version. Synthetic metrics are software tests,
> not evidence of expected market returns.

## Original research gate

| Relationship | Coint p-value | Half-life | Hurst | Beta instability | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| Oil / energy equities | <0.0001 | 9.3d | 0.16 | 5.4% | PASS |
| Copper / miners | <0.0001 | 13.1d | 0.26 | 8.6% | PASS |
| Gold / TIPS | <0.0001 | 13.4d | 0.22 | 32.2% | PASS |
| Credit / equity | <0.0001 | 7.0d | 0.12 | 12.3% | PASS |
| Banks / curve proxy | 0.9871 | n/a | 0.64 | 335.5% | REVIEW |

The rejected relationship contains a deliberate structural beta change. It is
diagnosed and reported but excluded from the portfolio.

## Reproduce the current version

~~~bash
atlas-rv research   --provider synthetic   --config configs/universe.yml   --observations 1500   --seed 7   --output reports/research
~~~

The new bundle includes the exact snapshot checksum, FDR q-values, cost attribution,
asset-class allocations, regime metrics, and a self-contained HTML report.
